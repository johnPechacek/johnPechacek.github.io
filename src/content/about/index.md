# About Me

The part of design I like most is the research end. I want to understand why someone makes a decision. I enjoy discovering what people actually need versus what they say they want. I have spent the last decade doing that for products I believe make a real difference in people's lives. Good education opens doors that would otherwise stay closed, and that kind of work drew me in at Istation. Though I have never hunted, I found the same pull at Moultrie, designing tools that get people into the woods and help them pay close attention to the natural world.

My background is in animation and motion design, and it shapes how I think about interaction. I don't just understand how movement can enhance an experience but when it can get in the way. I spent a year as a front-end engineer at Istation, building the kinds of designs I used to hand off. That changed how I think about design decisions and how I communicate those decisions to developers.

I live in Birmingham, Alabama with my wife, two boys, and a dog. I play video games and tell myself it counts as research.
